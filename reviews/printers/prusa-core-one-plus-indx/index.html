What we measured

Thirteen metrics from the printer protocol, plus nozzle hardness because this machine is marketed as abrasive-capable. Reliability batch of 12 prints: the standard calibration set, functional parts, and the toolchanger test file. Reference materials: our PLA and PETG batch, plus ASA because the machine is sold as high-temp capable.
