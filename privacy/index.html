Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Maker News Network ("we," "us," "our"), published by 3D Musketeers, LLC, collects, how we use it, and your choices. It applies to makernewsnetwork.com and any associated services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address; if you use a social login (Google, Discord), the basic profile the provider shares (email, display name, the provider's account id for you). A display name, derived from your email or your provider profile, is shown next to your comments.
  • IP address: collected at account signup and stored with your account, used specifically for abuse/spam prevention (see Section 2). This is personal information under some privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA) and is handled accordingly.
  • Comment/submission content: anything you post publicly or send us via the submission form or email, with the time it was posted. Removed comments are hidden, not erased, until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Account timestamps: when you signed up, when each verification step was completed, and when you last signed in.
  • Usage data: standard web analytics (pages visited, general location by IP region, browser/device type) via cookies - see Section 4.

We do not collect payment information directly (affiliate purchases happen on the retailer's site, not ours).

2. How We Use Information

  • Account verification: to reduce spam and coordinated inauthentic activity, new accounts go through an initial email/social verification and a second verification step 1-24 hours later. IP address and account creation timing are used as part of this process.
  • Comment moderation: to enforce our content standards (see Terms of Service).
  • Service operation: to deliver the Site, remember your login, and respond to support/legal requests.
  • Analytics: to understand aggregate site usage and improve content.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Social Login Data

If you sign in via Google or Discord, we receive basic profile information (email, display name) from that provider as permitted by their terms and your privacy settings with them. We don't receive your password for those services.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies for login sessions and basic analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser, but parts of the Site (like staying logged in) may not work correctly without them.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Site (hosting, email delivery, analytics) under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal compliance: when required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights/safety of the Site or its users.
  • We do not share your information with advertisers for their independent marketing use.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, including for the purposes of satisfying any applicable legal, accounting, or abuse-prevention requirements. When data is no longer needed for these purposes, we delete or anonymize it.

(Added 2026-08-01, modeled on the same vague-but-present formulation used by Construct3D/Seraph Technologies Ltd's UK GDPR privacy notice - no specific numbers committed, but the section exists rather than being absent entirely. Still worth revisiting with real numbers later if a specific issue makes it necessary - see below.)

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Contact us at legal@makernewsnetwork.com to make a request.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

9. International Users

The Site is operated from the United States. If you access it from outside the U.S., your information will be processed in the U.S., which may have different data protection laws than your country.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy; the effective date above reflects the most recent version. Continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the update.

11. Contact

Questions about this Policy or your data: legal@makernewsnetwork.com

Research: what do comparable outlets say about retention?

  • LTT Labs (closer in scale to this site): their published privacy policy doesn't state a specific retention period at all - no "Data Retention" section, no numbers. Vague-by-omission is apparently an acceptable real-world approach even for an established outlet, though not the most defensible one under strict GDPR reading.
  • Future plc / Tom's Hardware's parent: has a dedicated "Storage and retention of data" section, but it's part of an enterprise-scale, multi-brand, ad-tech-heavy privacy notice (LiveRamp, The Trade Desk, cross-brand data sharing, etc.) - several orders of magnitude more complex than this site needs, and the specific numbers weren't easily extractable (loaded behind an interactive accordion). Not a good direct model regardless - copying Future's scale of data-sharing disclosure would overstate what this site actually does with data.
  • Construct3D / Seraph Technologies Ltd (UK 3D printer manufacturer, checked 2026-08-01 since they're a real UK GDPR-bound company in the same product space): has a real "Data Retention" section but it's vague, not numeric - "as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes we collected it for, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements." No hard numbers. This is the formulation adopted in Section 6 above - a real, GDPR-facing precedent that "as long as necessary" is an acceptable, standard approach, not a cop-out. Also notable: Construct3D prints full legal entity name, physical address, phone, and a named DPO on the policy - standard UK transparency practice, not required for a US entity but worth knowing if the site ever seriously courts UK/EU readers.
  • Conclusion: adopted the vague/principle-based approach (Section 6) rather than hard numbers - closes the "section is completely absent" gap at no cost, while leaving specific figures (the 90-day/12-month/30-day style defaults floated earlier) as a future upgrade if a real situation calls for more precision.
