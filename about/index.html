About

By makers, for makers

Most coverage of 3D printers, scanners, CNC machines and lasers repeats the spec sheet. We built a lab to check it, and a newsroom that treats its readers like adults.

What this is

Maker News Network is an independent publication covering the tools makers actually buy: 3D printers and filament first, then 3D scanners, CNC and lasers. Reviews are built on measured data, re-tested at six months, and ship with the exact files we printed. News checks its sources. Comments open after launch.
