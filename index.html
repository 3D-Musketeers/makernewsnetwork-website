How reviews work here

Four rules we will not bend

  1. Measured data stays untouched

    Dimensions, decibels, watts, Rockwell hardness, pass or fail on a standard print. Recorded as-is. Opinion never edits a number.

  2. Opinion is labeled as opinion

    Ease of setup, software feel, whether a machine feels premium. Real and useful, and kept in its own clearly marked section.

  3. Per-metric scores, no overall score

    Every metric gets a 1 to 10 next to its number so you can see what we think it means. We do not roll them into one rating.

  4. We come back at six months

    Reliability, nozzle wear, firmware drift. The launch-week review is the start of the story, not the end.
